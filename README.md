# ATS Checker

**Upload your resume, get your score, and fix the gaps.** This app benchmarks `.pdf` and `.tex` resumes against specific job descriptions using a weighted 0-100 scoring algorithm. It identifies exactly where your profile fails the filter and provides direct, technical tips to optimize your content for modern tracking systems.

---

### Core Specs
* **The Filter:** Strictly `.pdf` and `.tex`. No `.docx` junk. We prioritize clean data over legacy formats.
* **The Pulse:** A 0-100 weighted algorithm that measures semantic intent, not just keyword density.
* **The Delta:** Raw feedback on **Strengths** vs. **Gaps**. No fluff, just the fix.
* **The Vibe:** Dark mode, neon accents, and glassmorphic UI. The interface glows **Emerald** when you're optimized and bleeds **Crimson** when you're failing.
